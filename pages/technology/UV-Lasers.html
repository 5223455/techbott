UV Laser

Introducing our cutting-edge UV lasers, designed to deliver exceptional precision and performance. These lasers emit ultraviolet light, making them ideal for a wide range of applications including scientific research, industrial manufacturing, and medical procedures. With their compact size and high energy output, our UV lasers offer unparalleled efficiency and reliability. Whether you need to engrave, mark, or cut materials with utmost accuracy, our UV lasers are the perfect solution. Experience the power of advanced technology with our state-of-the-art UV lasers.

Technical Specification

OPti-Vu5x UV Laser Marking System

The system is specifically designed for high-speed coding of HDPE and LDPE packaging materials while maintaining high-contrast readability.

Coding Capabilities

Text Marking: Alphanumeric codes, Production codes, Batch codes, Date codes, Expiry dates, Variable data

2D Codes: Data Matrix, QR Codes, Vision-readable codes, Serialized traceability codes

Traceability Features

  • Product lifetime tracking
  • Anti-counterfeiting coding
  • UDI compliance support
  • Permanent identification marking

The high uptime air-cooled laser source minimizes unscheduled downtime and eliminates routine consumable replacement.
