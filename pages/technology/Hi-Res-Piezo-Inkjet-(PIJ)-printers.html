Hi-Resolution Piezo Inkjet Printers

Introducing our advanced Hi-Resolution Piezo Inkjet (PIJ) printing systems, designed for high-definition coding and marking across multiple industries including medical, food, electronics, and building materials. Using imported PIJ nozzles for precise printing of QR codes, linear barcodes, date/time, batch/lot numbers, and more — ensuring clear, durable, and traceable identification on virtually any substrate.

Available in two powerful series — the TB Series with recirculating piezoelectric printheads for enhanced reliability, and the 8 Series with high-speed piezoelectric printing for maximum throughput.

Up to 600 DPI high-resolution printing

Speeds up to 240 m/min

Recirculating ink supply system

UV ink & oil-based ink support

SDK development kit available

Multi-angle installation support

Print on cartons, plastics, metal & more

Android touchscreen interface

PLC, RS232, RS485, Ethernet support

Auto ink type identification

Key Features

Advanced capabilities for every industrial printing need

SDK Development Kit

Supports multiple development languages (C#, C++, PHP, Python, Java) and various hardware interfaces (PLC, RS232, RS485, Ethernet) for easy integration.

PC Software

Message creating and editing for quick operation, device remote control, multiple printer synchronous management, and printing preview.

User-Friendly Interface

Extra-large industrial capacitive touchscreen with Android-based interface for instant printing and data editing.

Meet Customized Needs

Open platform and advanced system structure enable quick response to tailor-made various plug-ins and customized printing needs.

Ink Recirculation System

Ink recirculation at the printhead level ensures stable, high-quality printing. Removes impurities and bubbles, keeping nozzles in optimal condition.

Enhanced Environmental Adaptability

Longer throw distance prevents nozzle damage and dust interference. Stable printing on curved and irregular surfaces in harsh environments.
