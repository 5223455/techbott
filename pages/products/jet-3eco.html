Jet 3Eco Made in Germany

The Leibinger Jet 3Eco is a high-performance continuous inkjet printer designed for industrial marking and coding applications. It delivers reliable, high-quality printing with minimal maintenance requirements.

Factory-configured with one print head and a 3-meter umbilical

Equipped with over 800 intuitive, user-friendly functions

Supports printing of up to five lines of text

Sealtronic printhead system prevents ink drying and eliminates cleaning downtime

Contact-free printing at speeds reaching 403 m/min

Windows-based touchscreen interface for simple, efficient operation

Compatible with a wide range of ink formulations

Includes all software upgrades and enhancements at no extra cost

Why Leibinger?

German-engineered excellence with patented Sealtronic technology

Highest Operational Efficiency

With Sealtronic – Automated Nozzle Seal technology, no print head cleaning required daily. Instant start-up even after long shutdown periods of weeks and months – printer starts without cleaning the print head.

Lowest Operational Cost
  • 15%-20% lower solvent consumption vs competitors (Sealtronic technology)
  • No Hard Stop – No module change or core change required throughout printer life
  • Service interval: 12 months / 6000 hrs (Black ink) or 6 months / 3000 hrs (White ink)
  • Lowest power consumption: 40 Watt Max
Increased Productivity

Instant start-up even after long shutdown periods of weeks and months. No warm-up time, no cleaning cycles – just power on and print. Maximizes uptime and production output.
